Learning Guides

Meet the Characters

Educational companions designed to make abstract grammar concepts intuitive and memorable.

Subject Foundation

The Builder

Represents the Subject block. He lays down the foundation of who performs the action in every sentence.

Auxiliary Verbs

Helper

Supports sentence structure with Auxiliary verbs (do, does, did, can, will) to handle tense and questions.

Questions & Discovery

Pirry's Explorer

Guides question formation (Wh- words: Who, What, Where, Why) and encourages curiosity and pattern exploration.

Tenses & Timing

Time Mirror

Reflects the timeline and shows exactly when the action takes place (Past, Present, Future, Continuous).
